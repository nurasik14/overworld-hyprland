## Decisions

- **Sizes and slots** are in GUI px (`guiW`/`guiH` on `WidgetCard`, all cards 190 GUI px wide = 380 px at GUI 2). Slots are stacks in GUI px
  (`WidgetLayout`), so they follow `gui_scale` and the screen width. Left column ends at y 1052, right at 1024; the bottom-centre HUD area is
  free. The old herb card sat at the bottom centre, so the Block of the Day moved to the bottom of the left column.
- **Sign:** body = `quotes/quotes.json` (the original Quote behaviour, with attribution), corner splash = `quotes/splashes.json`
  (+ Mojang's list when present). The prompt said "message from quotes/splashes.json"; I kept both texts and made the splash the splashes.json part.
  Filed an offer to lore-writer to merge them if wanted.
- **Sun and moon** (`environment/celestial/*`) are drawn additively in the game (black = nothing), so `KeyedSprite` turns luminance into alpha with a
  3-line shader (`blackkey.frag`, compiled with `qsb`). They are 8×8-texel squares with a halo, like the game's.
- **Moon phase** is the real one (synodic month from the 2000-01-06 new moon); sun/moon travel an arc by the *location's* local hour.
- **Weather block mapping:** sand clear day · lapis clear night · sandstone/blue wool partly cloudy · light-grey wool overcast · smooth stone fog · water
  rain · snow block · packed ice sleet · gold block thunder. Columns are 6 GUI px blocks cut 1 texel = 1 GUI px from the real textures (`BlockPatch`).
- **Weather icons** (rain/snow/thunder/…) are original 16×16 pixel art generated by `assets/gen_weather_icons.py` (stdlib only), kept inside `Widgets/`.
- **Clock item:** frame 0 = noon, 32 = midnight (checked against the textures), index from the game's celestial-angle formula, 06:00 = tick 0.
- **Block of the day:** mobs show their spawn egg (all 26 exist), because entity textures are skin sheets; facts rotate one at a time (12 s) so the card
  stays 138 GUI px tall.
- **BrewTimer:** sounds go through `Theme.playSound` (`brew.done` brewing stand, `brew.drink` rest over) instead of `pw-play` + `brew.sound`; the old
  `brew.sound`/`fetch-minecraft-sounds.sh` plumbing is gone from QML (the CLI still has it: filed to qs-core). `notify-send` text is now
  "Brewing complete" / "Back to the crafting table".
- **Renames:** `ApoConfig` → `WidgetsConfig`, card id `herb` → `block` (IPC `widgets card herb` and old `widgets-hidden.json` entries still work), env
  `APO_WIDGETS_DEMO` → `OW_WIDGETS_DEMO` (old name still read). Nothing outside `Widgets/` used `ApoConfig`.
- **Fallback without Mojang assets:** flat colours instead of magenta for the frames, sign, weather/cava blocks; the item icons still show the missing
  texture (filed to asset-forge).
- **Preview-only env pins** in the shipped code (no effect unless set): `OW_WIDGETS_OPACITY`, `OW_WIDGETS_LAYER=top`.

## Not verified

- **No real pointer or keyboard input has reached the cards** (BRIEF §1 forbids moving the cursor): hover tooltips, button clicks, drag by the title
  bar, double-click reset, wheel-to-adjust on the brew clock, click-to-seek, click-for-next on the sign/block card. The handlers are unchanged
  Apothecary code or plain `MouseArea`/`McButton` wiring, and the state they drive is covered by the selftest.
- **The Jukebox buttons were never pressed**; the user's sleep music was playing in the browser throughout (real MPRIS data appeared on the card:
  title, artist, art, position).
- The Day/Night cross-fade while running (each process was pinned to one mode); GUI scale 3 and 4; other resolutions than 1920×1080.
- Screenshots in the compositor include the live Apothecary cards beneath (judge only my cards) and, at times, other agents' overlays. For that
  reason `qs-widgets-day/night.png` were taken with the card fill pinned opaque (`OW_WIDGETS_OPACITY=1`); the 0.72 look is in the `-translucent`
  offscreen renders (which lack the shader parts).
- Real Spotify / a player with a known length: the XP bar showed 0 because the browser stream reports no length.

## Bugs found on the way (all fixed)

digits collided with the slot's bright bottom edge; the hotbar selection sprite needs a 4 px nine-slice border; the sun sat under the grass line at
dawn; splash text overflowed the screen at the right edge; `shot.sh` recorded the wrapper subshell's PID instead of qs's.
